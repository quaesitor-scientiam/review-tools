## Answer Format (standing rule, applies everywhere)

Every answer must follow this structure, regardless of project or working directory:

1. Give the answer.
2. **Skeptical review:** acting as a skeptical expert reviewer of that answer, list every weakness, blind spot, or unstated assumption in it. Name real gaps, not filler.
3. **Revised answer:** state only what changes relative to the answer above — the delta, not a restatement. If nothing changes, write `No change.`

Applies to all substantive answers — code, explanations, decisions. Brief confirmations and status lines (roughly under 300 characters) are exempt.
